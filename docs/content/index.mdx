When a siren is **park killed** — silenced because the car was put in park while still in pursuit mode — tChirp chirps that car at intervals until someone takes a front seat. It never turns a siren on or off; it watches whichever ELS you run.

Free, MIT, not escrowed. No encrypted file and no public repository: the source is what you install.

## What it is not

- **Not an ELS.** No lighting, no siren control, no tones of its own.
- **Not a per-player sound.** Everyone near the car hears it.
- **Not a tone library.** Sounds are native GTA names, or a soundset you have.
- **One dependency.** [tLib](/docs/tLib) v1.0 or newer, which is free. Nothing else.

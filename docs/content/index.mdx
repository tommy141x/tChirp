![](/assets/tChirp/banner.jpg)

When a siren is **park killed** — silenced because the car was put in park while still in pursuit mode — tChirp chirps that car at intervals until someone takes a front seat. It never turns a siren on or off; it watches whichever ELS you run.

Free, MIT, not escrowed. No encrypted file and no public repository: the source is what you install.

## What it is not

- **Not an ELS.** No lighting, no siren control, no tones of its own.
- **Not a per-player sound.** It plays positionally from the vehicle, on every nearby client.
- **Not a tone library.** Sounds are native GTA names, or a soundset you have.
- **Not standalone.** Needs [tLib](/docs/tLib) v1.0 or newer, free.

## Upgrading from 1.0.0

<Callout type="danger" title="Check your config before upgrading to 2.0.0">
1.1.0 carried your values onto six renamed options for one release; **2.0.0 does not**. Lowercase the first letter of each: [Renamed, and no longer carried over](/docs/tChirp/configuration#renamed-and-no-longer-carried-over). `chirpAssignments` falls back to empty, so nothing chirps. `Config.LVCResourceName` was never read by any version — see [Settings that are gone](/docs/tChirp/configuration#settings-that-are-gone).
</Callout>
